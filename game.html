Explore green pharmacies, hospital campuses, glass terminals, roof gardens and four distinct old-town styles. Airports range from regional airfields to international hubs with twin runways and larger airliners. Traffic includes hatchbacks, vans, trucks, buses, ambulances and motorbikes. Vehicles follow lanes, brake for corners and give way at crossings. Buildings and vehicles are solid. Ease off before a corner, or hold the handbrake to slide. Grass slows you down. Dirt trails have looser grip and a lower top speed. Open M and use Find nearby to pin an airport, old town, highway or off-road area. Drive to earn spendable kilometres. Upgrade your car in the garage. Turbo recharges after use. While actively boosting (Shift + accelerate), hitting another car makes it explode and clears it from your path—including parked cars and police. Every police car you destroy awards 100 bonus kilometres to spend in the garage. Glowing pickups appear occasionally as you drive: speed boost (15s), ghost drive through every obstacle with full speed on grass (18s), and infinite nitro (25s; Shift + accelerate, even without a turbo upgrade). Collect them by driving over them. Ghost drive passes through buildings, traffic, police, planes, rocks and water. Cars you pass through while ghosting stay intact, even with nitro active. If ghost drive ends while you overlap an obstacle or vehicle, you can move clear before colliding with that object again. Choose Police chase for up to five coordinated pursuers: a chaser, an interceptor, two flankers and a unit aiming for junctions ahead. They close gaps at high speed, ram your car and intercept your path from ahead and both sides. Lost or destroyed units are quickly replaced ahead, outside your view. Switch modes any time to end the chase. Bus and truck jobs have no time limit. Bus jobs have 2–6 stops, including the first pickup and final drop-off. Each offer shows its total stops and bonus KM; the active job shows which stop you are on. Press E or Accept job to take an offer. Every new assignment needs your acceptance. Routes range from regional trips to cross-country journeys. Follow the marked bays and stop for two seconds to load or unload. Completed drop-offs earn bonus KM. Jobs and cargo wait for you when you switch modes, and are saved between sessions. Use Next job to get an offer with a pickup near your current location, even after roaming far away. You still need to accept it. Use End job to leave an unfinished assignment. The bright purple map route favours main roads, highways and longer straight stretches, avoiding old-town and airport shortcuts where practical. Short purple arrows on the road mark upcoming turns during accepted jobs. They appear earlier at speed, fade after each turn and leave long straight roads clear. Performance upgrades work on every vehicle; cosmetics stay on your free-roam and police-chase car.
